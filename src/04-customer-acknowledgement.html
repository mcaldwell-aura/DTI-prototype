Safety First

State to your applicant

  • Returning back to the office safely is our #1 goal
  • When I provide directions, that is not a command to make an immediate maneuver
  • You must take my directions and make a maneuver when it is safe to do so
  • You need to pay close attention to the road, traffic and pedestrians at all times
  • Left turns are the riskiest maneuvers during the test
  • When preparing to turn left, you must yield the right-of-way to oncoming vehicles, bicycles, or pedestrians
  • I ask that you use extreme caution and your best judgement while making a left turn

Ask your applicant: Do you understand the importance of what I just explained?

Road Test Maneuvers

Ask your applicant: When active emergency vehicles approach you, what side of the road must you yield to?

State to your applicant

  • If any driver-assist feature activates during the road test - allowing the vehicle to take over steering, braking, or acceleration - points will be assessed
  • During the road test you will demonstrate a parking maneuver
  • I will also observe your ability to safely operate a motor vehicle in most road situations
  • During the road test, continue driving straight unless I tell you otherwise
  • I will give you directions as far in advance as possible
  • If you do not understand my directions, please let me know
  • I will not ask you to do anything that is unsafe or illegal
  • You are responsible for the safe operation of this vehicle at all times
  • Your cell phone should be on vibrate and the radio off
  • Distracted actions such as eating, drinking, or smoking are not permitted
  • Our conversation will be kept at a minimum and will be limited to road testing only

Ask your applicant: Do you have any questions?
